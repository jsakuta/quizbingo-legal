Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This is an English translation of the Japanese original. If the two versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

The QuizBingo operator (the "Operator") handles personal information in the game service "QuizBingo" (the "Service") as described below, in accordance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws. Terms are as defined in the Terms of Service.

1Who we are

The business handling personal information is the QuizBingo operator. On request, we will promptly provide the Operator's name, address and the name of its representative. Contact details are on the Support page.

2Information we collect

The Service does not ask for your real name, phone number or address. The Operator collects the following information.

Entered byInformation
PlayersNickname, chosen avatar, relationship to the Guest of honor and team (optional), quiz answers and the time taken to answer, number of correct answers, bingo card and punched squares, messages, question ideas for the Guest of honor

  • Payment information: Payment details such as credit card numbers, and email addresses, are collected directly by the payment service providers (Stripe and RevenueCat) from the purchase screen. The Operator does not collect card numbers.
  • Storage on your device: The browser's storage (localStorage) holds your player ID, host key and Guest of honor key. They are used to return to a Party on the same device and are sent only in the Service's own communication.
  • IP addresses: To prevent misuse such as trying join codes by brute force, the Operator temporarily counts requests per source IP address in server memory. The Operator does not record or store them. Cloudflare, the platform the Service runs on, may handle IP addresses to process traffic and keep it secure.

3How we use it

  1. To hold Parties, run games, and show them on Players' smartphones and the venue screen
  2. To confirm Pass purchases and apply them to Parties, and to handle refunds and inquiries
  3. To prevent misuse (such as trying join codes by brute force)
  4. To check the information of the Party concerned when a problem is reported to us

4What other players see

Because of how the Service works, nicknames, avatars, relationship to the Guest of honor, teams, number of correct answers, time taken to answer, bingo order, messages and photos of the Guest of honor are shown on the screens of other Players in the same Party and on the venue screen. The Host can hide a Player's name or remove a Player from the Party.

5Retention and deletion

  1. A Party's information (including images) is deleted, together with the Party, 30 days after the later of the Party's last activity and the end of its Pass's validity period.
  2. Purchase records (amount, date and time, transaction number) are kept by the payment service providers under their own rules. The Operator refers to and keeps them for the period required by law for accounting and tax purposes.
  3. The content of inquiries and reply email addresses are kept as long as needed to respond, and deleted once they are no longer needed after we have finished responding.
  4. Images may remain in the browser cache of devices that viewed them.

On request, we will provide an outline of these measures.

7Data sent to third parties

The Service does not use cookie tracking, analytics tools or advertising. When you open the purchase screen for a Pass, your device communicates with RevenueCat and Stripe to process the purchase. The inquiry form opens on Google's site (Google Forms). In Japanese-language Parties, "Search Rakuten Ichiba" on the prize screen sends the words you enter from the Operator's server to the product search API of Rakuten Group, Inc. (no information about your device is sent). Images of the products in the results are loaded by your device directly from Rakuten's servers.

8Security measures

  1. All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
  2. Each Party's information is stored separately, and host controls are limited to devices that hold the host key. The host link can be reissued, after which the old link no longer works.
  3. We store only the information needed to run Parties, and Parties past their retention period are deleted automatically.
  4. Administrative operations on the Service are limited to the Operator.
  5. Information entrusted to providers outside Japan is handled with an understanding of the systems of the countries concerned.

9Minors, photos and sensitive information

  1. Players may include minors. Minors should use the Service with the permission of a parent or guardian. Real names are not required to join, and Players can join with a nickname that does not identify them.
  2. The Host uploads photos of the Guest of honor only with the Guest of honor's consent. If the Guest of honor asks for a photo to be deleted, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
  3. Please do not include sensitive personal information, such as medical history or beliefs, in messages, questions, question ideas, or facts about the Guest of honor entered for AI question suggestions.

10Requests for disclosure and other rights

We accept requests to notify you of the purpose of use, disclose, correct, add to or delete, stop using or erase, or stop providing to third parties the personal data we hold, and to disclose records of provision to third parties, through our contact details. With your request, please include the Party's join code and, so that we can confirm it is you, the information shown on screen: Hosts from the device that opened the host link, and Players from the device used to join. If we cannot confirm your identity, we may ask for more information. There is no fee. You can also ask the Host to remove you from the Party.

11Changes

If we change this Policy, we will post the changes and the date on this site.

Contact details are on the Support page.
