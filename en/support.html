Support

Contact us, report a problem, purchases and refunds, deleting your data

1Contact us

Please use the contact form. The form is in Japanese, but you can write your message in English. We will reply to the email address you enter in the form.

A reply may take a few days. If the Service stops working on the day of your party, don't wait for a reply: follow If something stops working. You can print paper bingo cards and a number list there.

2Reporting a problem

Including the following helps us investigate quickly.

  • The party's join code (4 characters)
  • When it happened, and which screen you were using (the host's screen, the big screen, or a player's smartphone)
  • Your device model, OS version and browser
  • A photo of the screen (if you have one)

3Purchases and refunds

  • A Pass is purchased for each party. For 2 months from the purchase date, you can play as many times as you like with the same party. It does not renew automatically.
  • Refunds are given under the conditions in Article 6 of the Terms of Service. When requesting one, include the party's join code and the date and time of purchase.
  • Refunds for purchases in the iOS app follow Apple's procedures.

4Deleting your data

  • A party's information is deleted automatically 30 days after the later of its last activity and the end of its Pass.
  • If you would like it deleted sooner, or would like a photo of the guest of honor deleted, please contact us. How we confirm your identity is described in section 10 of the Privacy Policy.
  • Players can also ask the host to remove them from the party.

5If you find an inappropriate name or post

The host can hide names and remove players from the player list on the host's screen. If you notice one from outside the party, please contact us with the join code.
